Keynote speakers

You do not need to be a professional speaker, and your topic does not need to be groundbreaking. Some of our best sessions have come from people talking about something they learned last month.

What we look for

A talk that teaches, sparks a conversation, or shows the room something you are excited about. First-time speakers are especially welcome, this is a friendly place to start.

Lightning talks welcome

Not ready for a full session? A five-minute lightning talk is a great way to dip a toe in. Bring one idea, one demo, or one story.

We will help with your slides

Tell us roughly what you want to cover and an organizer will help you shape it: an outline, a practice run, feedback on your slides, whatever is useful. You will not be left to figure it out alone.

Propose a talk

Send us your idea through our call for speakers form. Not sure yet? Say hello in the chat and we can talk it through.
